Process

How a project actually goes

No discovery theatre. The first paid milestone is small enough that both of us can walk away if the fit is wrong.

01

A call, then a one page scope

You describe the problem, I ask the questions that usually get skipped, and you get a short written scope with the first milestone, the price, and what is explicitly not included.

02

Working software every week

You see it running, not a status report. Weekly demo on the real thing, decisions made in the demo, and changes shipped the same week. If something is going to slip, you hear it early.

03

Live, then looked after

Deployment, monitoring, backups and the boring parts are part of delivery. After launch you can take it in house, keep me on a retainer, or both. Nothing is locked behind me.

Questions people actually ask

Before you message

How fast can you start?

Usually within one to two weeks, depending on what is already running. Small rescues can sometimes start the same week.

Do you work fixed price or hourly?

Fixed price per milestone, so the risk of my estimate being wrong stays with me. Retainers are monthly. I do not bill hourly, because it rewards the wrong behaviour.

Can you work with our existing team or codebase?

Yes, and a lot of my work is exactly that. I read the code first and tell you what I would keep, what I would leave alone, and what has to change before anything new goes in.

Who owns the code?

You do. It goes into your repository from the first commit, with your licence. I keep no hidden dependency on my own infrastructure unless you want me to host it.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. Several of the platforms in my work section are under NDA, which is why they are described rather than named.

What do you need from me to quote?

What the system has to do, who uses it, and what already exists. A rough call recording or a screenshare is more useful than a specification document.

Tell me what you are building

WhatsApp is fastest. Email works too. If it is a fit I will send back a short scope and a first milestone, not a sales deck.
